NOVA ROBOTICS INC. — ANNUAL REPORT SUMMARY (FISCAL YEAR 2025)

COMPANY OVERVIEW
Nova Robotics Inc. designs and manufactures autonomous warehouse robots for
mid-size logistics companies. Founded in 2018, the company is headquartered
in Austin, Texas, with a manufacturing facility in Guadalajara, Mexico.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
- Total revenue: $184.3 million (up 27% year-over-year)
- Gross margin: 41.2%
- R&D spend: $22.6 million (12.3% of revenue)
- Net income: $9.1 million (first profitable fiscal year)
- Cash and equivalents: $63.4 million

BUSINESS SEGMENTS
1. Hardware — autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and robotic arms, 68% of revenue.
2. Software — fleet management platform "NovaOS", sold on a per-robot
   subscription basis, 24% of revenue.
3. Services — installation, maintenance and training contracts, 8% of revenue.

KEY CUSTOMERS
Nova Robotics serves over 140 warehouse operators across North America,
including three of the ten largest third-party logistics (3PL) providers in
the United States. No single customer represents more than 9% of revenue.

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES FOR FISCAL YEAR 2026
- Expand into the European market via a new distribution partnership.
- Launch NovaOS 3.0 with predictive maintenance powered by on-device AI.
- Reduce hardware bill-of-materials cost by 15% through supplier
  consolidation.
- Grow the services segment to 12% of total revenue.

RISK FACTORS
- Reliance on a small number of semiconductor suppliers for robot
  controllers, creating exposure to component shortages.
- Increasing competition from larger industrial automation vendors entering
  the AMR market.
- Currency exposure from manufacturing costs denominated in Mexican pesos.
- Customer concentration risk in the logistics/e-commerce sector, which is
  cyclical.

OUTLOOK
Management expects fiscal year 2026 revenue growth of 20-24%, with gross
margin expansion to approximately 43% as manufacturing scale increases and
software mix grows.
