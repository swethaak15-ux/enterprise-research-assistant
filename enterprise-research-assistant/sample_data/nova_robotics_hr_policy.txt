NOVA ROBOTICS INC. — REMOTE WORK & TRAVEL POLICY (INTERNAL)

SCOPE
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Nova Robotics Inc. and its
subsidiaries.

REMOTE WORK
- Engineering, software and G&A staff may work remotely up to 3 days per
  week, subject to manager approval.
- Manufacturing, field-service and lab-based roles are required on-site.
- Employees must be available during core collaboration hours, 10:00-15:00
  local time, regardless of work location.

BUSINESS TRAVEL
- Domestic travel under $1,500 may be booked directly by the employee and
  expensed.
- International travel and any trip over $1,500 requires manager and
  finance pre-approval at least 10 business days in advance.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
  permitted for longer flights.

EQUIPMENT
- Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home-office stipend.
- Company laptops must be returned within 10 business days of employment
  termination.

REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations and Finance.
Last updated: January 2026.
